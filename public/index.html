About AgriSmart AI

Project Summary

AgriSmart AI is an applied machine learning and precision agriculture project developed by Sushmita Nandani for the IBM SkillsBuild Internship.

Core System Architecture

  • Supervised Learning Engine: Random Forest Classifier (100 estimators) trained on 2,200 empirical agro-climatic trial records across 22 crops.
  • Computer Vision Module: Chromatic leaf surface analyzer using the Excess Green Index (ExG) to segment lesions and diagnose foliar fungal blights and chlorosis.
  • Agronomic Reference: Guidelines, water management metrics, and soil parameters curated from Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).

Responsible AI & Ethical Notice

AgriSmart AI is an educational decision-support aid. It does not provide chemical pesticide prescriptions or guarantee commercial crop yields. Agricultural decisions should always be cross-referenced with certified local laboratory soil tests.
